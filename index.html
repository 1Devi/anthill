How to play

Foraging

Searching ants leave a blue trail that leads home. Ants carrying food leave a green trail that leads to food. Others follow these scents, so busy routes form on their own. Rain washes trails away.

Economy

The resource bar shows food brought in and spent per minute: every ant, larva and the queen eat all the time, and eggs and new chambers cost food too. The queen turns food into eggs: egg → larva → pupa → ant. Workers kept inside nurse the brood faster and dig.

The nest

Switch to the Nest view to dig: Granaries store food, Nurseries hold more brood, Living Quarters raise the population cap, Fungus Gardens grow food when workers tend them, the Infirmary heals ants at the nest, Guard Posts protect the queen during raids.

Research

Dig a Scent Hall, then open Research (R): stronger bites and armour, faster legs, keener antennae, longer trails, bigger loads, a more fertile queen, gardens and Deep Tunnels, which widen the area you can dig.

Danger

Spiders hunt your ants — swarm them and they become a feast. Rival colonies grow too and will raid your nest. If your queen dies, the game is over; destroy every rival queen to win.

Commands

Drop food — a pile of sugar (recharges). Scent trail — paint a food trail; ants follow it in the direction you drag. Rocks / Erase — reshape the terrain. Rally point — send soldiers somewhere; place it on a rival nest to raid it.

Controls

Right-drag or WASD to pan, wheel to zoom, Tab switch view, 1–6 commands, Space pause, F cycle speed, P trails, R research, C chronicle, L save / load, M mute, Esc back to Inspect. Click the minimap to jump around.
